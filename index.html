I’m Saransh — a freelance video editor and digital creator based in Delhi, India. I turn raw footage and ideas into sharp, story-led videos that hold attention and help brands make a lasting impression.

From short-form reels to brand films, commercials and motion-led edits, I bring together thoughtful storytelling, precise pacing and attention to detail. My goal is simple: make every second on screen count.
